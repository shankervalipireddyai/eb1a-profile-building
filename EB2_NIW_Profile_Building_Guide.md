## NIW in 60 seconds (Dhanasar framework)

Unlike EB-1A's 10-criteria checklist, NIW is granted when you prove three prongs:

1. **Substantial merit AND national importance** — your proposed endeavor matters to the US (not just your employer)
2. **Well positioned to advance the endeavor** — your record shows you can actually do it
3. **On balance, waiving the labor certification benefits the US** — your contributions justify skipping the PERM process

No job offer or employer sponsor required — same self-petition flexibility as EB-1A.

## What differs from EB-1A

- **Future-oriented, not just past achievements.** EB-1A asks "are you at the top of your field?" NIW asks "will your *proposed endeavor* benefit the US?" You need a clear **proposed endeavor statement** — e.g., advancing agentic-AI infrastructure for reliable autonomous systems in US enterprise and critical sectors.
- **Lower evidentiary bar, but not low.** "Well positioned" is easier than "extraordinary," yet USCIS still wants citations, adoption, expert letters, and impact metrics — the same evidence base.
- **No criteria checklist.** There is no "3 of 10." Instead, every piece of evidence should feed one of the three prongs. A scattered record fails; a record organized around the endeavor succeeds.
- **National importance ≠ national security.** Agentic AI reliability, AI infrastructure efficiency, and large-scale data systems all qualify as nationally important through economic competitiveness and technological leadership — you don't need a government contract.
- **Your EB-2 priority date (Sep 18, 2017) is an asset.** Two approved I-140s exist on that date; NIW preserves your place in line strategy — discuss portability with counsel.

## NIW-specific additions (small lift beyond the EB-1A plan)

- **Proposed endeavor statement (1–2 pages):** write it early — it organizes every NIW exhibit. Frame: "developing risk-adaptive agentic-AI infrastructure for reliable autonomous enterprise systems in the United States."
- **Future-impact letters:** ask 2–3 recommenders to add a paragraph on what your *future* work will contribute to the US (NIW-specific; EB-1A letters focus on past acclaim).
- **Endeavor-aligned publishing:** the ContinuityAgent line and USENIX '27 submission already align with the proposed endeavor — keep them there.
