# EB-1A / EB-2 NIW / O-1 Profile-Building Resources

A working toolkit for building an extraordinary-ability immigration profile — assembled for my own EB-1A preparation and shared publicly.

## What's inside

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `EB1A_Profile_Building_Guide.md` | 6-month action guide: criterion-by-criterion activities ranked by evidentiary weight, what USCIS wants at step 1 vs. final merits, evidence checklists, and common traps |
| `EB2_NIW_Profile_Building_Guide.md` | Companion guide: Dhanasar prong mapping, EB-1A vs. NIW differences, and activities that do double duty for both petitions |
| `EB1A_Profile_Building_Opportunities.xlsx` | Database of profile-building opportunities (hackathon judging, speaker CFPs, reviewer calls, award panels, podcasts, trade publications), mapped to EB-1A criteria |
| `EB1A_Contacts_Database.xlsx` | Public contacts: conference organizers, journal editorial offices, hackathon organizers, award programs, AI podcasts, trade publications |
| `EB1A_AAO_Decision_Reviews.md` | Reviews of USCIS AAO decisions — what wins and the recurring reasons petitions are denied |
| `EB1A_Video_Resources.md` | Curated public videos: EB-1A recipient interviews and law-firm strategy webinars |
| `Shanker_Valipireddy_Resume.pdf` | Current resume |

## Notes

- AAO non-precedent decisions are **persuasive only, not binding** — the reviews are study material, not legal advice.
- All opportunities and contacts were verified as real at the time of compilation; deadlines and links change, so verify before acting.
- Databases are refreshed regularly; check the commit history for updates.
